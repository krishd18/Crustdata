Six-step workflow
one dossier per target · steps can be skipped

1FIND

In
A thesis in plain words ("acquirable logistics in MH, $5M+ revenue")
Out
Ranked shortlist; every signal cites its field and date; filters shown, unsupported criteria listed
Search + enrich11–19 credits

2SCREEN

In
The company (name or domain)
Out
Early red flags from its public claims; registry entities (CIN, status); news analysis; public documents
Web + news≈13 credits

3REACH OUT

In
Recipient you were given, your team, the purpose
Out
An intro asking for a teaser or deck, sent exactly once
Approval0 credits

4CHECK

In
Their deck: PDF, scanned PDF (OCR), PPTX, link, text or JSON request
Out
Claims register: verdict, one-line reason, source, value, date; inconsistencies across sources
Records + models≈19 credits

5REQUEST

In
Every claim the records couldn't settle
Out
"Ask them for this": one document per claim (EPFO, GST, leases, contracts), sent exactly once
Approval0 credits

6RE-CHECK

In
The step 4 snapshot
Out
What changed: verdicts, records, headcount, job posts, each leader's own profile; optional founder watch
≈19 creditsWatch: price first
